The door opens on sealed hoppers.

Three food-grade chambers hold the powders. No open scoops, no shared tubs.

How it works

Three steps.
One cup.

01

Choose your supplement

Pick a product and a flavour, then set the number of label servings. The grams and the per-serving price show before you pay.

02

Pay for your serving

Pay from the demo wallet, UPI or card. You are charged per scoop — one label serving is the smallest unit.

03

Collect your measured scoop

The auger drops the dose into a fresh cup, the load cell verifies it, then the tray unlocks. Shake and drink.
